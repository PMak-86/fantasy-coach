Fantasy Coach is a personal, non-commercial fantasy basketball analytics project designed to help one manager make more informed lineup, matchup, waiver and trade decisions across standard and dynasty leagues.

Planned coaching features

Data-driven recommendations tailored to each league's scoring settings and competition format. These capabilities are planned and not yet live.

↗

Weekly lineup optimiser

Compare eligible players, projected availability and scheduled games to recommend the strongest weekly-locked roster.

◫

9-category matchup forecasts

Analyse opponent rosters and projected category strengths to identify opportunities in head-to-head matchups.

⌕

Waiver wire insights

Find potentially useful available players based on roster needs, schedules and category contribution.

⇄

Trade and dynasty evaluations

Assess current-season impact while considering longer-term player value in dynasty competition.

Built for two league formats

A single private dashboard is planned to manage two existing Yahoo Fantasy Basketball leagues associated with one account.

STANDARD

Weekly competition

Short-term optimisation focused on getting the best output from each week's fixed starting lineup.

  • Lineup recommendations
  • Category-by-category predictions
  • Schedule and waiver evaluation
DYNASTY

Long-term roster building

Balance this season's chances with the future outlook of developing players and established veterans.

  • Player age and development outlook
  • Multi-season trade evaluation
  • Prospect and keeper tracking

Planned Yahoo Fantasy integration

Fantasy Coach is seeking approval to use the Yahoo Fantasy Sports API. The proposed integration will access only the league information required to generate personal fantasy basketball analysis.

No Yahoo account connection or live Yahoo data access is implemented on this website.
